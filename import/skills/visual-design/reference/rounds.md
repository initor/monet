# Rounds

## Diverge

- Give every designer the same brief and a different lens: the artifact's purpose, its story or history, the reader and the page it sits on, or a craft (type, color, motion). In the logo work, four stand-ins given prescriptive directions drew the same four symbols.
- Run the designers independently and in parallel, one agent each, with no view of each other's work. When independent lenses arrive at the same concept, say so on the page: it is the strongest signal a round gives.
- Write each concept as one line: the object, what it does, and why it fits the brief. A concept that needs a label to be understood is not ready.
- Before a round, dry-run the brief cold: two or three fresh agents read only the brief and say what they would ask. Define every word they stumble on. Stop after two passes.
- One skeptic per option, run in parallel with a time box, tries to refute it: is it honest, does it read at the smallest real size, does it look like a stock icon, does it hit something the owner dislikes. Its points become flags on the card, not a verdict. A single skeptic for every option ran past 80 minutes on a page overview and never reached the third option.
- Prefer a distinctness floor to a high average. An average across lenses favors familiar forms.

## Converge

- Rebuild the picked option as named parameters in a generator in `tools/`, and check that it reproduces the pick before changing anything.
- Sweep one knob at a time. Each variant changes one parameter, so each card answers one question.
- Give every number a reason, and put the reason in the chip's tooltip.
- A polish pass changes craft, not identity. When the owner doubts the identity, go back to a round.

## Panels

A judge panel may score concepts, to write the round notes and to order the work. Its scores never pick, never rank the page, and never reach the owner as a recommendation. When the owner's pick and the panel disagree, the owner's pick stands.

## Time

Tell the owner how long a step takes before starting it, and offer a lighter version. A lighter round keeps three lenses and one skeptic per option, and drops the cold dry-run and any judge panel. On a page overview, a lighter round's designers took 7 to 13 minutes each. From the logo work, at maximum effort: a concept round with one study agent, three designers, a judge, and a skeptic ran about an hour. A build with a spec, tooling, a builder, three reviewers, and a fixer ran about 2.4 hours.
